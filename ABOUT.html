About Hands of Hope Foundation

Hands of Hope Foundation is a community-focused charitable organisation created to help people facing poverty, unemployment, homelessness and other difficult circumstances.


We believe meaningful change happens when communities work together. Our programmes focus on immediate support while also creating opportunities for long-term independence.

Our Vision

A society where every person has an opportunity to live with dignity, hope and purpose.

Our Values

Compassion

We care about people and their circumstances.

Dignity

We treat every person with respect.

Integrity

We act honestly and responsibly.

Community

We believe that we are stronger together.
